### [RETAILER DESCRIPTION — Page 3, full text]

A distinctive and somewhat overlooked name from the British guitar scene, Maverick guitars were designed with one goal in mind: to offer serious performance, innovative features and outstanding playability without the premium price tag.

Established in the late 1990s, Maverick developed a reputation for its unconventional designs, modern hardware and close association with renowned guitar hardware designer Trev Wilkinson. Built in Korea to British designs, these instruments offered an interesting alternative to the familiar Ibanez, Jackson and ESP guitars of the era.

This Maverick Custom carries that same adventurous spirit, offering something refreshingly different from the usual electric guitar. With its distinctive character and roots in an innovative but relatively short-lived guitar brand, it's a fantastic choice for players who appreciate unusual instruments with a story behind them.

An underrated piece of early-2000s guitar history, well worth discovering!
